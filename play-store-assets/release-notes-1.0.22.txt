GPS Driver 1.0.22

- Nearby chargers now load from the phone's current location even before a trip starts.
- Trip start can use a planned stop, a typed destination, a map-selected point, or no destination.
- Plan My Day supports recurring weekdays and direct trip start from each scheduled leg.
- Voice destination entry now reports permission, recognizer, timeout, and recognition errors clearly.
- Route guidance and charging recommendations are stored as auditable snapshots.
- Notifications can open the relevant plan or trip without starting collection automatically.
